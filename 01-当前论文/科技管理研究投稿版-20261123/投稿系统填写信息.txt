【中文题名】
创新药评价的部门注意力配置差异与协同治理

【英文题名】
Differences in Departmental Attention Allocation in Innovative Drug Evaluation and Collaborative Governance

【中文摘要】
创新药上市审评、医保准入与临床使用分别由国家药品监督管理局、国家医疗保障局和国家卫生健康委员会负责，三部门评价规则的衔接影响企业研发证据准备与创新成果转化。基于“制度逻辑—组织注意力”分析框架，采用经人工核验的大语言模型编码，对三部门2018—2026年发布的51个政策来源的4 481条句段进行编码，比较安全性、有效性、经济性、创新性和公平性五维注意力配置，并结合政策条文与访谈资料考察评价对象、比较药品和结论用途。结果表明：药监局安全性与有效性注意力占比合计为68.38%，医保局经济性注意力占比为76.48%，该格局经多种稳健性检验保持稳定；卫健委安全性与经济性注意力占比较高，但二者排序受联合发文影响；药监局创新性注意力占比为20.96%，高于医保局的5.84%和卫健委的3.94%；同一评价维度在不同部门服务于不同决策任务，既有研发证据的跨环节使用须考虑评价对象、比较药品与结论用途。跨部门协同并非统一评价标准，而是明确评价结论的适用条件，提高评价规则可预期性与证据使用效率。据此提出通过前置沟通、补充研究统筹和临床应用反馈3条协同路径，为优化支持创新药的政策组合和促进成果转化提供参考。

【英文摘要】
Innovative drugs are subject to sequential evaluations for market authorization, health insurance access, and clinical use by the National Medical Products Administration, National Healthcare Security Administration, and National Health Commission, respectively. Coordination of these evaluation rules directly affects enterprises' preparation of evidence during R&D and the translation of innovation outputs. Drawing on an institutional logics–organizational attention framework, this study uses a large language model validated against human coding to code 4,481 passages from 51 policy sources issued by the three departments between 2018 and 2026. It compares attention allocation across five dimensions—safety, efficacy, economic value, innovativeness, and equity—and examines evaluation objects, comparators, and uses of conclusions through policy provisions and interview materials. The results show that safety and efficacy together account for 68.38% of the attention allocated by the National Medical Products Administration, while economic value accounts for 76.48% of that allocated by the National Healthcare Security Administration; these patterns remain robust across multiple checks. The National Health Commission allocates relatively more attention to safety and economic value, although their ranking is sensitive to jointly issued documents. Attention to innovativeness is higher at the National Medical Products Administration (20.96%) than at the National Healthcare Security Administration (5.84%) and the National Health Commission (3.94%). The same dimension serves different decision tasks across departments, and the cross-stage use of existing evidence requires checking evaluation objects, comparators, and uses of conclusions. Cross-departmental coordination should therefore clarify the conditions under which evaluation conclusions apply, rather than unify evaluation standards, so as to improve the predictability of evaluation rules and the efficiency of evidence use. Accordingly, three pathways are proposed: early communication, coordinated supplementary studies, and feedback from clinical use. These findings provide a reference for optimizing the innovation policy mix and translating innovation outputs.
